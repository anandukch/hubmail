Hubmail Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Hubmail ("we", "our", "the service") is a multi-account Gmail connector that lets you search, read, and manage email across one or more Gmail accounts through AI assistants and other tools that support the Model Context Protocol (MCP). This page explains what data Hubmail accesses, how it is used, and how it is protected.

Data we access from Google

When you connect a Gmail account to Hubmail, you grant access to the following Google OAuth scopes:

  • gmail.readonly — read your email messages and labels
  • gmail.modify — move, label, delete (trash), and draft messages, and create labels and filters, on your explicit request
  • gmail.settings.basic — read and manage basic Gmail settings (e.g. filters)
  • openid / userinfo.email — identify your Google account email address during sign-in

You can grant read-only access by deselecting write permissions on Google's consent screen; in that case, write-capable actions (delete, draft, label, filter creation) are disabled for that account until you reconnect with write access.

How we use your data

Hubmail uses Google user data solely to provide the features you explicitly request: searching your mailbox, reading a specific message, and performing the write actions listed above when you ask an AI assistant to take them. Email content is fetched live from the Gmail API for each request and is not stored by Hubmail. We do not use your email content or metadata for advertising, and we do not sell or share it with third parties, except as necessary to operate the service (e.g. calls to the Gmail API itself).

Hubmail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we store

  • Your account details: email address and a bcrypt-hashed password (if you sign up with a password).
  • Your Gmail OAuth refresh token, encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM, used to obtain short-lived access tokens on your behalf.
  • Connected account aliases (e.g. "work", "personal") you assign when connecting a Gmail account.
  • An audit log of tool calls made against your account — which tool was used, which account alias, the parameters involved (e.g. a search query string or a message ID), the requesting IP address, and a timestamp. This log does not contain email body content or attachments.

We do not store the body, attachments, or full content of your email messages. Each read or search request is served live from the Gmail API and returned directly to the requesting client.

Data retention and deletion

Your account data, encrypted tokens, and audit logs are retained until you delete your Hubmail account or disconnect a Gmail account from the dashboard. You can also revoke Hubmail's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page, which immediately invalidates our ability to access that account.

Security

  • Gmail refresh tokens are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM.
  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and never stored in plaintext.
  • Your personal MCP connector URL contains a random, unguessable token — treat it like a password, since anyone with it can call the tools on your behalf.
  • All traffic to Hubmail is served over HTTPS.

Children's privacy

Hubmail is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
